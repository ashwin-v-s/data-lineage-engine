## Limitations

1. **Semi-automated ground truth.** Truth labels are human-derived propagation maps
   (ReferenceInterpreterProvider), not machine-verified ProvSQL traces.
   See `docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md` Entry R-01.
2. **10-case SQL subset.** Results cover PROJECTION, ARITHMETIC, CASE_WHEN, SUM,
   JOIN, COUNT(*), WHERE, COALESCE, SELECT*, CASE_WHEN_all_branches only.
3. **Mixed granularity not tested.** AQ-4: DATASET and COLUMN level predictions
   are scored separately per protocol; no cross-granularity cases in this set.
4. **No column-level runtime evidence.** OpenLineage events are dataset-level;
   the runtime evidence used here is synthetic (constructed from GT labels).
5. **Plumbing-mode numbers excluded.** The W03 mock fixture (8-unit plumbing)
   results are NOT reported here. Only real benchmark cases are included.
